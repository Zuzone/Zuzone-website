From a page to a planet.

Zuzone used to be a newsletter page with a house icon. Now it is a living mark and a live globe of 24 hubs and pop-up cities. The reel tells it in three movements: the page, the mark, the world.

The cut

Every cut lands on a tock. Old-site shots are desaturated and flat. The mark and the platform are full cream, forest and lime. Captions carry the story so the reel works muted. The player carries a guide voice (an AI read, for timing only); the bed below it has no voice, for your own recording.

Bed and metronome

The metronome is one soft wooden tock every 0.8 s from the first frame to the last, so every edit point sits on a beat. Under it, a 60 s instrumental bed at 75 BPM: felt piano and muted keys, no drums. The bed drops out for one bar at 0:12 and returns with the first dot at 0:14. A low air whoosh under the globe reveal at 0:30, one bright ding on the last tick at 0:58.

Voice-over, to record

Nine lines, 68 words. Calm, unhurried, one idea per line, a slight smile on the last. Start each line at its time; the budget is the room it has before the next cue. “Zuzone” is ZOO-zone. Record one line per file or one take with a second of silence between lines, 48 kHz mono, and the lines drop onto the bed.

How each shot was made

  1. Old site. zuzone.org recorded in headless Chrome at 810×1440, scrolled frame by frame at 30 fps, graded flat (saturation down, light vignette). The logo is a 4× crop of the live header.
  2. Brand plates. A 1080×1920 motion page built from the identity's own geometry: twelve dots on a 137-unit ring, the Cormorant "Zu" glyphs, lime marker, 0.8 s tick. Rendered deterministically frame by frame, so every tick is exact.
  3. Platform. The live site in a 540×960 phone viewport on a Retina window (1080×1920 device pixels, screencast at the browser’s own frame rate), driven by script: two globe drags, a pin tap, the Gantt strip, the Zupulse thread, the Events panel, typing in the newsletter strip, the Historic Brief scroll. The globe spin is motion-interpolated to 30 fps.
  4. Transition. Seedance 2.0 through monid, first frame pinned to the old page, last frame pinned to the globe screen, 720p 9:16, 5 s, audio off.
  5. Captions rendered from the same page as the plates (DM Sans on a cream card, lime underline), faded in and out over 0.2 s. Assembly in ffmpeg: identical encode per segment, concat, caption overlays, then the audio mix.
