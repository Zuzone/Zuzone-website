Bed and metronome

The metronome is one soft wooden tock every 0.8 s, first frame to last. Under it, a 58 s bed at 75 BPM: felt piano and a soft plucked synth, no drums. A low air whoosh under the globe at 0:03, one bright ding on the last tick at 0:57.

Voice-over, to record

Twelve lines, 92 words. Warm, a touch quicker than the reel: this one invites. Start each line at its time; the budget is the room it has before the next cue. “Zuzone” is ZOO-zone, “Zupass” is ZOO-pass. One line per file or one take with a second of silence between lines, 48 kHz mono, and the lines drop onto the bed.

How each shot was made

  1. Brand plates. A 1080×1920 motion page built from the identity's own geometry: twelve dots on a 137-unit ring, the Cormorant "Zu" glyphs, lime marker. The hook ticks every 0.36 s, the lockup every 0.8 s; the tagline card and end card are the same page. Rendered deterministically frame by frame.
  2. Platform. The live site in a 540×960 phone viewport on a Retina window (1080×1920 device pixels, screencast at the browser’s own frame rate), driven by script: two globe drags, a pin tap, the Gantt strip, the Zupulse thread, the Events panel, typing in the newsletter strip, the Historic Brief scroll. The globe spin is motion-interpolated to 30 fps.
  3. The door in. A second short capture: the List view under the globe, then Events → “Add a village”, which opens the Zupass sign-in modal. Nothing was signed in or submitted.
  4. Captions rendered from the same page as the plates (DM Sans on a cream card, lime underline), faded over 0.2 s. Assembly in ffmpeg: identical encode per segment, concat, caption overlays, then the mix.
