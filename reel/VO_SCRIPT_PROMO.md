## Plain text, for the teleprompter

One world. Twelve months. Places to belong.
Twenty-four independent hubs and pop-up cities, on one shared map.
Each hub is its own thing: its own identity, network, projects and affiliations.
Zuzone is the shared zone, where they connect, exchange knowledge, join Town Halls, and support one another.
Every village: when it runs, where it is, how to join.
A year of pop-ups, on one timeline.
The pulse of the network: every village's posts, in one thread.
Monthly notes from the hubs, in your inbox.
Born from the spirit of Zuzalu.
Add your village. Sign in with Zupass.
One tick. A new place.
You can belong to your own thing, and still be in the zone. Zuzone. zuzone dot org.

## Recording notes

48 kHz mono, quiet room, 20–30 cm from the mic, one line per file (v01 … v12) or one take with a second of silence between lines, 2–3 takes each. Lines 3 and 4 are the longest; keep them conversational, not read. Line 12 is the ending: slow down on "and still be in the zone", then the name.
